Our Story

Sol was born for
this neighbourhood

We started Sol Café because we knew the struggle — students pulling all-nighters with nowhere warm to sit, elderly neighbours wanting a calm place to read, young professionals needing fast coffee without the queue. Sol is the answer to all of it.

Every table is designed with purpose. Study zones come with power outlets, USB charging, and desk lamps. We have dedicated accessible seating near the entrance, and a quiet corner that feels like your own office.
